Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal information Finshra may collect when you use the website or apply to the partner programme. Only provide information through an active official form and do not email sensitive identity documents unless specifically requested through a verified secure process.

1. Information collected

Depending on the feature used, information may include name, mobile number, email address, city/state, referral code, verification status, registration status and payment reference details. Do not provide Aadhaar, PAN, bank credentials or other sensitive information unless it is necessary, legally justified and requested through a secure process.

2. Why information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and administer partner registration.
  • To verify a mobile number where OTP verification is enabled.
  • To maintain payment, support, fraud-prevention and compliance records.
  • To communicate programme changes and respond to requests.

3. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the website, verification, hosting or payment processing, and with authorities where required by law. Payment credentials should be handled by the payment provider, not stored in plain text by Finshra.

4. Security and retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information. Records are retained only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations, dispute handling or legitimate business records. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Your requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, subject to legal record-retention requirements. Contact ronitshraddha@gmail.com.

6. Children

The paid partner programme is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Do not submit a partner registration if you are under 18.
