Frequently asked

Know before you join.

Does registration guarantee work or income?

No. Opportunities depend on availability and eligibility. Registration does not guarantee work, customers, commissions or income.

Can partners earn by recruiting paid partners?

No. The programme is designed around qualifying product or service referrals and other defined activity, not rewards for recruiting another paid partner.

Do workers need to pay to register?

No. Worker registration is intended to be free under this model. The planned ₹25 fee is for partner registration only.

When can I register?

Partner registration is planned to open soon. The registration process will display the full terms before any payment is requested.
