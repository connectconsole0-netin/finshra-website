Important information

Know what to expect

Finshra does not promise fixed monthly earnings, guaranteed jobs, leads or commissions. Results depend on eligible activity, customer interest, availability and the programme terms. Do not treat partner registration as a job offer or investment.
Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Can I earn money from home with Finshra?

The programme offers referral activities that may be carried out remotely. Any commission depends on qualifying referrals and the applicable terms. Income is not guaranteed.

What is the registration fee?

The one-time partner registration fee is ₹25. Review the terms and refund policy before registering.

Is Finshra a salaried work-from-home job?

No. The partner programme is a referral-based opportunity, not a salaried employment position.

Do workers need to pay to register?

Worker registration is free under the programme information currently published by Finshra.

Is income of ₹20,000 per month guaranteed?

No. Finshra does not guarantee ₹20,000 per month or any fixed income. Actual commissions, where applicable, depend on eligible activity and programme conditions.
