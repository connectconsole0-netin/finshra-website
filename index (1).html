What does the ₹25 fee cover?

The final launch terms must clearly define the membership/onboarding benefits, duration, taxes where applicable, and refund/cancellation policy. Do not pay until these are published.

Is registration and payment live?

No. This is a static website prototype. The form does not transmit or save personal data, and there is no payment gateway connected.

How will my information be handled?
