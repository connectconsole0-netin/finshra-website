Transparency first

Questions, answered clearly.

Is the ₹25 fee recurring?

No. The partner registration fee is a one-time ₹25 fee, subject to the published programme terms.

Does registration guarantee work or income?

No. Opportunities depend on availability and eligibility. Registration does not guarantee work, customers, tasks, commissions or income.

Can I earn by recruiting other paid partners?

No. Partners do not earn simply by recruiting other paid partners. Commission is tied to qualifying product or service activity under programme rules.

Do workers need to pay to register?

No. Worker registration is free under this programme model. The ₹25 fee applies only to partner registration.

Where can I get help?

Email support@consoleconnectindia.online for support. Do not pay an individual outside the official checkout.
