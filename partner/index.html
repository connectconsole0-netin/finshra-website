Frequently asked

Know before you join.

Does registration guarantee work or income?

No. Opportunities depend on availability and eligibility. Registration does not guarantee work, customers, tasks, commissions or income.

Can partners earn by recruiting paid partners?

No. Commission is tied to qualifying product/service referrals or other specifically described activity, not merely recruitment of paid partners.

Do workers need to pay to register?

No. Worker registration is intended to be free under this model. The ₹25 fee is only for partner registration.

When can I pay the fee?

Do not pay outside the verified checkout.
