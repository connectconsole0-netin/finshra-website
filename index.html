02 · Partner network

A separate programme with defined participation rules.

Partners may share information about eligible Finshra products or services and connect interested local workers with the free worker-registration route when available. Partner registration is not a job offer and does not guarantee earnings.

What partners may do

  • Share approved information about eligible products or services.
  • Refer potential customers under published referral rules.
  • Direct interested workers to the free worker-registration route when available.
  • Receive commission only for qualifying activity described in the written terms.

What the programme is not

Partners do not earn simply by recruiting other paid partners. Participation does not guarantee customers, tasks, commissions or income.

Eligibility, qualifying actions, payout timing and any limits are explained in the partner terms.

FAQs

Clear answers.

Does Finshra provide training courses?

This website focuses on Finshra’s technology services and partner programme. It does not advertise courses or promise training that is not currently offered.

Does paying a registration fee guarantee work or income?

No. Registration does not guarantee a job, customers, tasks, commissions or income.

How can partners earn?

Only through eligible product or service referrals and other qualifying activity described in the written partner terms. Rewards are not paid merely for recruiting another paid partner.

How does Finshra handle personal information?

Before registration opens, the registration process will provide a privacy notice explaining what data is collected, why it is needed and how to request support.
