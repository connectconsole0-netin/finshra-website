Refund Policy

This policy applies to the proposed ₹25 one-time partner registration fee. Please do not make a payment unless the official checkout is enabled on this website and the fee and terms are shown before confirmation.

1. Duplicate or failed transactions

If your account is debited but the payment status remains failed, or if you are charged more than once for the same registration, contact us with the transaction reference. We will review the payment record and arrange correction or refund where the charge is confirmed as duplicate or unsuccessful.

2. Registration not activated

If a payment is confirmed but partner registration is not activated, contact support with the payment reference. We will verify the transaction and either complete the registration if eligible or process an appropriate refund.

3. Other refund requests

For other cancellation or refund requests, contact support promptly. Eligibility will be assessed based on the status of the registration, the disclosed terms, applicable law and payment-provider rules. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

4. How to request support

Email support@consoleconnectindia.online with your registered mobile number, date/time and payment reference. Never send your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or banking password.

5. Processing time

After a refund is approved, the time for the funds to appear depends on the payment provider and your bank. We will provide an update when the request has been reviewed.
